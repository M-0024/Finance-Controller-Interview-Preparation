# 05 — Analysis Frameworks & Ratio Glossary

## The Five-Lens Company Analysis Framework

When asked *"How would you analyze a company?"*, structure the answer this way — it reads as Controller-level structure, not memorized definitions:

> "I would analyze the company from five perspectives: profitability, liquidity, efficiency, leverage and cash flow."

| Lens | What You Check |
|---|---|
| **Profitability** | Revenue growth, Gross Margin, EBITDA Margin, EBIT Margin, Net Profit Margin |
| **Liquidity** | Current Ratio, Quick Ratio, Cash position |
| **Efficiency** | DSO, DPO, Inventory Turnover |
| **Leverage** | Debt-to-Equity, Net Debt/EBITDA, Interest Coverage |
| **Cash Flow** | Operating, Investing, Financing, Free Cash Flow |

## The Controller's Step-by-Step Analysis Process

```
Step 1 — Validate the data (Trial Balance, reconciliations, adjustments)
Step 2 — Prepare financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
Step 3 — Analyze profitability (Revenue, Gross Margin, EBITDA, EBIT, Net Profit)
Step 4 — Analyze Balance Sheet (Working Capital, AR, AP, Inventory, Debt, Cash)
Step 5 — Perform variance analysis (Actual vs Budget vs Forecast vs Prior Year)
Step 6 — Identify root causes of material/unusual variances
Step 7 — Provide management insights: what happened, why, financial impact, recommended action
```

## Full Ratio Glossary

**Profitability**
| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Gross Margin | Gross Profit ÷ Revenue × 100 |
| EBITDA Margin | EBITDA ÷ Revenue × 100 |
| EBIT Margin | EBIT ÷ Revenue × 100 |
| Net Profit Margin | Net Profit ÷ Revenue × 100 |
| ROE | Net Profit ÷ Equity |
| ROA | Net Profit ÷ Total Assets |

**Liquidity**
| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Current Ratio | Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities |
| Quick Ratio | (Current Assets − Inventory) ÷ Current Liabilities |

**Efficiency**
| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Inventory Turnover | COGS ÷ Average Inventory |
| Receivables Turnover / DSO | Average AR ÷ Revenue × No. of Days |
| Payables Turnover / DPO | Average AP ÷ COGS × No. of Days |

**Leverage**
| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Debt-to-Equity | Total Debt ÷ Equity |
| Debt-to-EBITDA / Net Debt/EBITDA | (Total Debt − Cash) ÷ EBITDA |
| Interest Coverage | EBIT ÷ Interest Expense |

## Other Analysis Types Worth Knowing (Broader Analyst/BA Context)

| Type | What it evaluates |
|---|---|
| **Market Analysis** | Industry size, growth, competitors, market share, trends |
| **Marginal Analysis** | Additional revenue vs additional cost of one more unit/decision (MR > MC → profitable) |
| **Cost-Benefit Analysis** | Expected benefits vs expected costs of a decision (e.g. automation ROI) |
| **Trend Analysis** | Performance over multiple periods — is it rising, falling, or flat? |
| **Competitor Analysis** | Head-to-head comparison on revenue, margin, market share |
| **SWOT Analysis** | Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats |
| **Break-Even Analysis** | Point where Total Revenue = Total Cost; Break-even units = Fixed Cost ÷ (Price − Variable Cost) |

## Three Equations to Have Instantly Ready

```
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
EBITDA − D&A = EBIT
PBT − Tax = Net Profit
```
